# Healthy Habit Challenge

One small habit, offered as a challenge the reader can start today and keep for a week.

Headline: `🎯 THIS WEEK'S CHALLENGE` (no weekday).

Open by naming the habit in one line, then say how long it takes. If it takes more than
five minutes a day, it is the wrong habit for this channel.

Sections that work well:

- `🌱 THE CHALLENGE` — exactly what to do, in two or three steps.
- `💡 WHY IT WORKS` — the reason, in plain language, in two or three lines. No
  physiology lecture, no percentages, no studies.
- The contrast block.
- `📅 HOW IT LOOKS THIS WEEK` — a few `☑️` lines showing it is the same small thing
  repeated, not a programme that escalates.

The habit must be free, need no equipment, and survive a busy day. If someone forgets
on Tuesday, the post should already have told them Wednesday is fine — say so, warmly,
in one line. A challenge nobody can restart is a challenge nobody finishes.

Never set a target that is a number on a scale — no weights, no measurements, no
counting calories.
