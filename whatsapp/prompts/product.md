# Product Highlight

One Grillo product, introduced briefly. Keep it minimal — this is a channel about food,
and a product post that reads like an advertisement costs the channel its credibility.

Headline: `🥗 ON THE SHELF TODAY` (no weekday).

The product's details are given below. Work only from those details. Do not invent a
sourcing story, a farm, a process, a nutrition figure or a claim that is not there.

Sections, and this type stays close to this shape:

- `🌱 WHY THIS ONE` — two or three lines on what it is and what it does for a meal.
  Plain and specific: fills you up, is easy to carry, keeps for two days in the fridge.
- `🍽️ HOW TO EAT IT` — three or four genuinely easy ways to use it, each one line.
  This is the part people keep.

No contrast block and no challenge on this type.

Never mention price, offers, stock or where to buy. Never say *best*, *premium*,
*finest* or *our special*. Never compare it to another brand. No urgency of any kind —
nothing runs out today.

Health claims are the thing to be most careful with. "High in protein and fibre" is a
fact about the food. "Controls your sugar" is a claim about a person, and it does not
go on this channel.

This is the shortest type on the channel. Three sections plus the closing is plenty.
