# Format exemplar

The post below is the house style. Read it for **structure, rhythm and register** —
how sections are separated, how lists are set, where the closing furniture goes, how
warm the voice is.

Do not read it for length, and do not reuse its sections. Two things in it are specific
to this one post and are **not** the house pattern:

- **Its per-reader sections.** `👵 FOR ELDERS` and `👶 FOR YOUNG ONES` are particular to
  this post. Posts are not split into a block per reader. A post for a mixed household
  serves everyone inside its ordinary sections, in the same lines the rest of the post is
  written in.
- **Its length.** Eight sections is at the very top of the channel's range. Most posts
  are three or four, and the sections a post gets come from its own type file.

Do not reuse its wording either. `Simple. Light. Nutritious.` is that post's own
three-word line; yours is written fresh for the post you are writing.

---

🌅 WEDNESDAY MORNING BREAKFAST

Good Morning, Everyone ☀️

Young or old. Busy or relaxed.

One simple habit that works for all ages:

Eat something within 1 hour of waking.

Your body has been resting all night. It needs a gentle start.

—

👵 FOR ELDERS

Start your day with something warm and light:
🥣 Moong dal soup
🥛 Warm milk with a pinch of turmeric
🍌 Mashed banana
🍚 Soft idli or upma

These are easy to digest and gentle on the stomach.

—

👶 FOR YOUNG ONES

Keep it fun and simple:
🥗 Sprouts with lemon and salt
🍌 Banana + peanut butter
🥣 Moong dal chilla
🍎 Apple slices
🥚 Boiled egg

Simple food. Full of energy.

—

🔄 INSTEAD OF THIS… TRY THIS

Instead of skipping breakfast…
→ Eat something small — a fruit, a handful of sprouts

Instead of heavy or oily food in the morning…
→ Choose light and warm food

Instead of eating alone…
→ Sit together as a family
→ Share a meal
→ Talk, laugh, and eat slowly

Instead of drinking tea or coffee on an empty stomach…
→ Drink warm water first
→ Have your tea or coffee after eating

Instead of eating while watching TV or phone…
→ Sit at the table
→ Look at your food
→ Enjoy the moment

Instead of rushing through breakfast…
→ Take 5 minutes
→ Sit down and eat slowly

—

🌿 FOR THE FAMILY

One simple breakfast:
☕ A warm drink
🥗 Something fresh
🍚 Something light
🍎 Something nourishing

It doesn't need to be fancy. It just needs to be real.

—

🎯 TODAY'S CHALLENGE

☑️ Sit together as a family (even for 5 minutes)
