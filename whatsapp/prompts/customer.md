# Customer Story

A post built around something a real customer actually said or did.

Headline: `💚 FROM OUR CUSTOMERS` (no weekday).

**Everything factual in this post comes from the topic supplied below.** That topic is
the real detail, collected by the Grillo team from an actual customer.

Never invent:

- a name, an age, a place, or a family member
- a length of time ("for the past six months…")
- a quantity, a weight, a measurement, or a test result
- a health outcome of any kind — no "her sugar came down", no "he lost weight", no
  "she sleeps better now"

An invented testimonial is a fabricated review, and one of those does more damage to a
channel than a hundred posts do good. If the detail you were given is thin, write a
thin post. Do not fill the gap.

**If no specific story was supplied**, do not manufacture one. Write the post in the
plural and unattributed instead — "Several of you have told us…", "A question we get at
the shop every week…" — describing a situation the reader recognises, with no named
person, no quoted speech and no outcome. That is honest, and it still works.

Sections that work well:

- The situation, in two or three lines. A real kitchen problem: no time in the morning,
  children who won't eat vegetables, cooking for one.
- What changed, described as a practical adjustment rather than a result.
- `🌿 IF THIS SOUNDS LIKE YOU` — two or three lines the reader can act on.

No contrast block and no challenge on this type.

The point of the post is the reader recognising themselves, not the customer being
impressive. Warm, ordinary, small.
