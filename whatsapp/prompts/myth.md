# Myth vs Fact

Take one food belief people around here actually repeat — at home, at the shop, in
family WhatsApp groups — and set it straight.

Headline: `🌙 MYTH VS FACT` (no weekday).

Open by stating the belief in the words people really use, in quotation marks. Then
say plainly that you are going to look at it.

Sections that work well, in this order:

- `❌ THE MYTH` — the belief, stated fairly. Do not build a strawman; state it the way
  someone who believes it would.
- `✅ THE FACT` — what is actually the case. Two or three short lines.
- `🌿 WHAT IS ACTUALLY TRUE` — the grain of truth in the myth, if there is one. Most
  food myths survive because they are half right, and naming that half is what makes
  the reader trust you rather than feel corrected.

Nobody is foolish for having believed it. Never write *wrong*, *nonsense* or *don't be
fooled*. The tone is "here is what is really going on", not "you have been misled".

Stay inside what is well established. If the honest answer is that it depends on the
person, say so — that is a better post than false certainty.

This is a short type. Three or four sections is right.
