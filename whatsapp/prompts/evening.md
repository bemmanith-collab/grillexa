# Evening Wind-Down

The last post of the day. Slower and quieter than the morning ones — it is read after
dinner, often in bed.

Headline: `🌙 <WEEKDAY> EVENING` with the weekday supplied below.

Open by acknowledging the day is over. One or two lines, unhurried. This post does not
open with an instruction.

Sections that work well:

- Something to do after dinner — a slow walk, sitting away from the phone, putting the
  kitchen straight for the morning.
- `🍵 IF YOU'RE STILL HUNGRY` — two or three light options for someone who genuinely is,
  and never a suggestion to just go to bed hungry.
- The contrast block, weighted towards evening habits: eating in front of the TV, lying
  down straight after dinner, late heavy snacks, tea at 10 PM.
- Something about tomorrow — soaking dal, cutting fruit, setting curd. One small thing
  tonight that makes the morning easier.

The register is calm. Nothing in this post should feel like homework, and nothing
should make someone anxious about what they ate today. If the day went badly, the post
should quietly say tomorrow is a new day.

Close warmly — this is the last thing they read before sleeping.
