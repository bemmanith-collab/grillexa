# Evening Wind-Down

The last post of the day. Slower and quieter than the morning ones — it is read after
dinner, often in bed.

Headline: `🌙 <WEEKDAY> EVENING` with the weekday supplied below, or
`🌙 TONIGHT'S WIND-DOWN RITUAL` when the post is about the routine rather than the meal.

Open by acknowledging the day is over — two or three short lines, unhurried, no
instruction in them. Something like *"Before you sleep… take 5 minutes for yourself. Not
for work. Not for your phone. Just for you."*

**The angle this type owns: tonight shows up tomorrow morning.** Most people judge a
dinner by how it tasted. The more useful test is how the next morning feels — whether you
wake up light or heavy, whether you are hungry at seven or have no appetite until eleven.
Say it plainly and stay inside ordinary experience: cooked vegetables at night are light
and easy to digest, so you wake less heavy; a heavy, oily, late dinner is still sitting
there at sunrise. Do not turn it into physiology, and do not claim it fixes anything.

## Sections — all four, in this order

This type has a fixed shape. Include every one of these, every time.

**1. `🧘 3 THINGS TO DO BEFORE BED`** — exactly three, numbered `1️⃣` `2️⃣` `3️⃣`, each with
a one-word heading in capitals and two or three short lines under it. One of the three is
always about food or drink; the other two are about winding down — breathing, putting the
day away, thinking of one good thing, setting the kitchen straight for the morning. Keep
each one small enough to do in under two minutes.

**2. `💤 WHAT TO AVOID`** — a short `❌` list, five or six items, one line each. Late
caffeine, a heavy or fried dinner, sugary drinks, screens in bed, lying down straight
after eating, overthinking the day. No explanation per line; the list carries itself.

This is the one place the channel lists things not to do, and it works here because it is
about the hour rather than about the person. Keep it that way — nothing in this list is
ever about someone's body, their weight, or what they ate today.

**3. `🌿 WHAT TO EAT IF YOU'RE HUNGRY`** — three or four light options with emoji, one
line each. Never suggest going to bed hungry: if someone is hungry at 10pm they should
eat something small, and the post should say so warmly.

**4. `🎯 TONIGHT'S CHALLENGE`** — four `☑️` items, each doable tonight without buying
anything. End at least one of them with a way out — *"Sleep by 11 PM (or whenever works
for you)"* — so nobody who reads this at midnight has already failed it.

Do **not** include the `🔄 INSTEAD OF THIS… TRY THIS` block on this type. `WHAT TO AVOID`
does that job here, and both together makes the post twice as long as an evening post
should be.

## Register

Calm. Nothing in this post should feel like homework, and nothing should make anyone
anxious about what they ate today. If the day went badly, say quietly that tomorrow is a
new one. Close warmly — this is the last thing they read before sleeping.
