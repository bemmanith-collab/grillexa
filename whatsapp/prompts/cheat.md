# Sunday Cheat Meal

Sunday is when families cook something special or order in — biryani, dosas with extra
ghee, bajji in the evening, sweets, ice cream after. Nobody is giving that up, and this
post does not ask them to.

The job is to make the same meal better, not smaller. The reader should finish this post
looking forward to Sunday, not feeling watched.

Headline: `🍛 SUNDAY CHEAT MEAL`.

Open by putting Sunday's meal where it belongs — part of a good week, not a break from
one. One or two lines, warm, no conditions attached.

Sections that work well:

- `🍽️ THE SUNDAY FAVOURITES` — name what people around here actually eat on a Sunday.
  Be specific and be affectionate about it. This section is not a warning list.
- `✨ THE BETTER VERSION` — the same dish with small changes. A bowl of raita and sliced
  onion next to the biryani. One extra vegetable in it. Shallow-fried instead of deep.
  Curd rice at night after a heavy afternoon. Sharing the sweet instead of one each.
  Every suggestion keeps the dish recognisable — nobody wants a biryani that has been
  improved into something else.
- The contrast block. This is the heart of this type, and it is where most of the value
  is, so give it real swaps people will actually make.
- `🌿 AFTER THE MEAL` — a slow walk, water, a lighter dinner if lunch was heavy.

Never write, in any form:

- that the meal has to be earned, burnt off, worked off, walked off or made up for
- that Monday is for fasting, skipping, "getting back on track" or a detox
- calories, weights or numbers of any kind
- that they should not eat it, or should eat less of it because of how it looks

The difference between this post and a diet post is one idea: **a good week has a Sunday
in it.** If a line in your draft only makes sense in a week that does not, cut it.

This type is always written for Sunday, whatever day it is generated on.
