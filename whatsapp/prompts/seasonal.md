# Seasonal Food

What is good to eat right now, in this season, in this part of the country. The season
is given below.

Headline: `🍋 EATING IN <SEASON>` or similar, fitted to the season (no weekday).

Open with one line on what this season does to people — the heat, the damp, the chill,
the festival cooking. Then the food that answers it.

Sections that work well:

- `🌾 WHAT'S GOOD NOW` — fruits and vegetables actually in season here, with one line
  each on how to use them. Name what a local market has this month, not what a
  supermarket stocks all year.
- `🥤 SIMPLE THINGS TO MAKE` — two or three easy preparations.
- The contrast block, if the season has an obvious habit worth swapping — cold drinks
  in summer, fried food during festivals, skipping water in winter.

Seasons here, roughly: hot and dry March to May; monsoon June to September; a mild
festival season October and November; cool December to February. Write for the one
named below.

Stay away from anything that reads as folk medicine. Seasonal food is about what tastes
good and is cheap and fresh right now, not about warding anything off.
