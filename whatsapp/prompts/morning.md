# Morning Tips

A day-to-day tip for the start of the day. This is the channel's most-read slot — it
arrives while people are still deciding what the day looks like.

Headline: `🌅 <WEEKDAY> MORNING` with the weekday supplied below.

Open with a greeting and one habit stated in a single line, plain enough to remember
without re-reading. Then one line on why it helps — the reason, not a lecture.

Sections that work well:

- The habit itself, broken into two or three concrete steps.
- A short list of foods or actions that fit the habit.
- The contrast block.
- `🎯 TODAY'S CHALLENGE` — two or three `☑️` items, each doable before lunch today
  without buying anything.

One habit per post. A post carrying three habits gets none of them followed.

The habit should cost nothing and need no special ingredient. Drinking water before
tea, sitting down to eat, eating within an hour of waking, chewing slowly, keeping cut
fruit where people can see it — that is the register.
