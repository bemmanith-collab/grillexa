# Dinner, general audience, ridge gourd

Reference only. **Nothing in `examples/` is sent to the model** — the exemplar that ships
with every request is `prompts/example-post.md`. This folder is for whoever edits the
prompt files next, to see what the current instructions are aiming at.

Written by hand, not generated. It is a target, not a sample of output.

```
npm run generate -- --type=meal --slot=dinner --ingredient="ridge gourd"
```

Three things it is meant to demonstrate:

1. **The everyday ingredient earns a section, not a mention.** `🥒 TONIGHT, THE
   BEERAKAYA` plus a line in the swaps. An ingredient named once in passing is rotation
   that does not show.
2. **The night-to-morning link is the spine.** It appears in the hook, in `⏰ TWO HOURS`,
   and again in the last line of the challenge — the argument for a light dinner, not a
   footnote to it.
3. **A mixed audience is served inside ordinary sections.** The elders appear in one
   clause of the beerakaya section. There is no `👵 FOR ELDERS` heading, because posts
   are not split into a block per reader.

---

🌆 THURSDAY EVENING DINNER

Dinner has one job.

Not to be the best meal of the day — just light enough that you sleep well and
wake up wanting breakfast.

Tomorrow morning starts tonight.

—

🥒 TONIGHT, THE BEERAKAYA

Ridge gourd is sitting in most kitchens and gets cooked the same one way.

It takes under ten minutes:
🍲 Beerakaya curry, thin, with a little dal
🥘 Cooked with moong dal and a spoon of ghee
🌿 The peel, which usually goes in the bin, dry-fried with garlic and chilli into
   a chutney
🍚 Either one, with two chapatis or a small plate of rice

Soft and quick to cook, so it suits the elders at the table without a separate
dish being made.

—

🍽️ OR ANY OF THESE

If there's no beerakaya in the house:
🥣 Thin dal with rice
🍲 Vegetable khichdi
🥛 Curd rice
🍚 Two chapatis and whatever vegetable was cooked

Cooked and warm beats raw and cold at night. Easier to digest, and you feel it in
the morning rather than at 11 PM.

—

⏰ TWO HOURS

Finish about two hours before you lie down.

That single gap does more for how tomorrow morning feels than swapping any one
dish for another.

—

🔄 INSTEAD OF THIS… TRY THIS

Instead of dinner at 10 PM…
→ Eat by 8, even if it's smaller

Instead of a lunch-sized plate at night…
→ Half of it is enough

Instead of raw salad late…
→ Cooked vegetables — lighter on the stomach

Instead of leaving the vegetable out because it takes time…
→ Beerakaya is done in ten minutes, less than the rice takes

Instead of lying down straight after…
→ Ten slow minutes on your feet, kitchen tidied

Instead of skipping dinner to make up for a heavy lunch…
→ Eat something small and light — going to bed hungry wakes you at 3 AM

—

🎯 TONIGHT'S CHALLENGE

☑️ One cooked vegetable on the plate
☑️ Finished by 8 PM
☑️ Ten minutes upright before you lie down

Notice tomorrow morning. That's the whole point.

—

💬 GRILLO SAYS

"A light dinner is not about eating less. It is about waking up tomorrow feeling like eating breakfast."

Cooked. Light. Early.

🥗 Know Your Food Better.

— Grillo
